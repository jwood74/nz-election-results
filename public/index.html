About these numbers

Results are read from the election XML feed and checked every minute. Party votes and candidate votes are separate contests. Seat allocations are reproduced from the feed, not independently modelled. Early results can be unrepresentative, and final results include special votes.

Open an electorate for candidate results, party votes and polling-place detail. Party-vote change uses saved final 2023 results; electorate-wide comparisons are limited to officially unchanged boundaries. No predictions are included.
